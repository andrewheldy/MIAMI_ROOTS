## Purpose

Captures the founding intent and values behind Miami Roots, so product, design, and
operational decisions can be checked against "does this fit who we are," not just "does
this work."

## What belongs here

- The core ideas Miami Roots is built on
- The tone/feeling the community and brand should evoke
- What Miami Roots deliberately avoids being

## What does not belong here

- Concrete brand assets, color, or typography decisions (see
  `docs/04-design/brand-foundation.md`)
- Specific copy or voice rules (see `docs/04-design/content-and-voice.md`)
- Product features (see `docs/01-product/product-scope.md`)

## Known initial information

These are seed concepts as given at project founding — recorded here deliberately
unpolished, to be refined by whoever owns brand/product rather than locked in prematurely.

- **Strong communities.** The network is worth building because dense, real communities
  are worth more than shallow, wide ones.
- **Real relationships**, not transactional networking. Miami Roots is not a business-card
  exchange.
- **Collaboration over isolation.** People do better — in business, health, and life — with
  each other than alone.
- **Members are greater together than the sum of their parts.** The value of the network
  compounds as it grows, if it grows in a healthy way.
- Miami Roots should help people **share opportunities and contribute value**, not just
  extract it.
- **Community growth should improve the experience for everyone** — growth that dilutes
  quality or trust is growth in the wrong direction.
- **"Together we rise"** is a load-bearing thematic idea — it should show up in brand
  language and in how features are designed (e.g., rewarding contribution, not just
  invitation volume).
- The brand should feel **warm, vibrant, welcoming, optimistic, distinctly Miami, and
  community-first.**
- Explicitly **avoid**: corporate networking clichés, transactional behavior, exclusionary
  language, and forced positivity.
