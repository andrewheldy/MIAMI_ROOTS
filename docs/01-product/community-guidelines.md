## Purpose

States the behavioral norms Miami Roots expects from members, derived from the project's
ethos, as a reference for both product copy (e.g. onboarding, group descriptions) and
moderation practice.

## What belongs here

- Plain-language expectations of member behavior
- Group-specific rules that are part of the product's public promise (e.g. no ticket
  reselling in Nightlife & Event Marketing)

## What does not belong here

- How moderation is actually carried out day to day (see
  `docs/05-operations/moderation.md`)
- Legal terms of service (not started — flag as a research item in
  `docs/06-research/research-backlog.md` if pursued)

## Known initial information

Derived from `docs/00-context/vision-and-ethos.md`:

- Be a real participant, not a lurker looking only to extract value. Share opportunities
  and contribute, don't just take.
- Collaboration over isolation — help other members grow rather than treating them as
  competition.
- No corporate-networking-style transactional behavior (e.g. cold pitching, spam,
  mass-DMing group members).
- No exclusionary language or behavior — the community grows by welcoming people in, not by
  gatekeeping.
- Be genuine — avoid forced positivity or performative engagement; real relationships over
  appearances.

### Group-specific rules

- **Nightlife & Event Marketing:** no ticket reselling. Ticket resale is explicitly
  directed to a possible future, separate group (see
  `docs/00-context/community-groups.md` and `docs/01-product/out-of-scope.md`), not this
  one.
- **Sober Social:** this is a peer social space for sober people who want to participate in
  Miami's social life — it is not a treatment or clinical recovery service, and should
  never be presented or moderated as one.
