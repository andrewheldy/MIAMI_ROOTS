## Purpose

Index for planning documents: what's still unresolved, and the rough sequencing of future
work.

## What belongs here

- `open-questions.md` — unresolved questions that block or shape future decisions
- `roadmap.md` — phase-level sequencing of future work

## What does not belong here

- Settled decisions (see `docs/07-decisions/decision-log.md`)
- Detailed delivery/implementation planning for the current phase (see
  `docs/08-delivery/implementation-plan.md`)

## Relationship to other documents

Planning documents feed into `docs/07-decisions/decision-log.md` once a question is
resolved, and into `docs/08-delivery/implementation-plan.md` once work is ready to be
sequenced concretely.
