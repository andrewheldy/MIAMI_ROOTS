## Purpose

Translates the project's ethos into practical guidance for writing copy — onboarding text,
group descriptions, admin-facing labels — so tone stays consistent as different people
write it.

## What belongs here

- Voice attributes and what they mean in practice
- Words/patterns to avoid, with the reason
- Example phrasing, where useful

## What does not belong here

- Visual identity (see `docs/04-design/brand-foundation.md`)
- The underlying values themselves (see `docs/00-context/vision-and-ethos.md` — this doc
  is downstream of that one)

## Known initial information

Derived directly from `docs/00-context/vision-and-ethos.md`. No copy has been finalized —
this is guidance for whoever writes it.

### Voice should be

- **Warm** — like being welcomed by a person, not processed by a form.
- **Vibrant and optimistic** — energy appropriate to Miami and to a community people
  actually want to be part of.
- **Distinctly Miami** — local, specific, not generic "community app" language.
  Community-first phrasing: "we," "together," "rise" over "users," "platform,"
  "leverage."
- **Direct and honest** — especially around Sober Social (never clinical or
  treatment-toned) and ticket rules (state the "no reselling" rule plainly, not
  apologetically).

### Avoid

- **Corporate networking clichés** — "synergy," "leverage your network," "unlock
  opportunities" — replace with plain, human phrasing about people helping people.
- **Transactional framing** — avoid language that makes referrals/points feel like a
  quota or a sales funnel to the member reading it, even though internally it is tracked
  that way.
- **Exclusionary language** — nothing that implies who doesn't belong; the community grows
  by welcoming people in.
- **Forced positivity** — don't oversell with exclamation-point-heavy, hype-y copy;
  confidence and warmth read better than forced enthusiasm.
