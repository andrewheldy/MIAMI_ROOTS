## Purpose

Index for research-related documentation: what needs investigating before it can be
designed or decided, and where any sources consulted are tracked.

## What belongs here

- `research-backlog.md` — questions that need external research (not just internal
  decision-making) before they can be resolved
- `source-register.md` — sources consulted during research, so claims can be traced back

## What does not belong here

- Internal product/architecture questions answerable from within the team (see
  `docs/02-planning/open-questions.md`)
- Settled decisions (see `docs/07-decisions/decision-log.md`)

## Relationship to other documents

Research backlog items that get resolved should feed into either
`docs/00-context/assumptions.md` (working assumption) or
`docs/07-decisions/decision-log.md` (settled decision), citing the source in
`source-register.md`.
