## Purpose

Index for the decision log — the durable record of material product and architecture
decisions and their rationale.

## What belongs here

- This index only. The actual log lives in `decision-log.md`.

## What does not belong here

- The decisions themselves (see `decision-log.md`)
- Unresolved questions (see `docs/02-planning/open-questions.md`) or working assumptions
  (see `docs/00-context/assumptions.md`) — those move here once actually decided

## Relationship to other documents

Any material decision made while working in this repository — a new dependency, a schema
shape, an auth approach, a third-party integration choice — should be recorded in
`decision-log.md` at the time it's made, per `CLAUDE.md` / `AGENTS.md`.
